# Code of Conduct


中文版：[`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Purpose

Agent-Top welcomes clear, kind, and constructive collaboration.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific feedback tied to the contribution.
- Protect learners from shame, harassment, or gatekeeping.
- Keep examples safe and call out risks clearly.
- Distinguish opinion, fact, and assumption in technical discussions.

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Attack, discriminate against, harass, or threaten others.
- Publish private information, secrets, customer data, or production incident details.
- Substitute non-actionable personal judgments for code or content feedback.
- Let framework preference override clear trade-offs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove hostile, spammy, or harmful behavior. Repeated violations may lead to contribution bans.

### Handling

If you encounter a problem, report it to a maintainer. Maintainers will handle it according to community size and circumstances, prioritizing the protection of contributors and learners.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to GitHub, Discussions, Discord, workshops, hackathons, and other Agent-Top community spaces.
