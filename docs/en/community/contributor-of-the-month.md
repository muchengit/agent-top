# Contributor of the Month

## Purpose

Recognize contributors who shipped meaningful documentation, Labs, translations, reviews, or maintenance work.

## Eligibility

- Merged tutorials, Labs, interview questions, translations, or maintenance fixes.
- Maintained or improved CI, templates, or contribution workflows.
- Helped unblock other contributors.
- Kept quality standards high, including examples and review feedback.

## Selection

- Maintainers nominate before the month ends.
- Contributors and reviewers may add evidence.
- Core team confirms the winner.

## Benefits

- Badge in the next community showcase.
- One month of reduced review overhead for low-risk docs PRs.
- Priority visibility in release notes or roadmap reviews.
